WE ARE LIGHT ENGINEERS

We place a lot of emphasis on the provenance of our materials, which are all locally procured, machined and assembled in the UK. We are fully in control of our supply chain, so any component we manufacture can be made out of any metal alloy and are all 100% recyclable.

MODULAR DESIGN

The modular design optimization method in the design of LED lamps can improve production efficiency, reduce costs and improve product quality. Through modular design, LED lamps can achieve independent design and optimization of light source, power supply, connection and auxiliary functions, and enjoy the advantages of improved production efficiency, reduced maintenance costs, convenient product upgrades and high quality controllability. With the continuous development of LED lamps market, modular design will play an increasingly important role in LED lamps design.
